    A grey tile with a "?" means this project hasn't assessed that country yet — most of the world's ~195 countries aren't in this dataset. That's different from "no AI-specific governance," which means it was checked and nothing was found.

    International & regional frameworks

    These apply across many countries at once rather than to a single territory, so they're listed here instead of shaded on the map. The European Union is the one exception — its 27 member states are shaded on the map with EU AI Act data, since it is binding, country-specific law.

    Every row in this map was independently fact-checked — a first research pass, then a second, separate verification pass that looked for at least one additional independent source before anything was published. A small number of rows couldn't be fully resolved; those are flagged below rather than hidden.
