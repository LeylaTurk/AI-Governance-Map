# Batch E — Middle East

Research date: 2026-09-22

## Overview

Four rows: Israel, Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Qatar. None of the
four has a single comprehensive national AI statute. All rely on a mix of
general (non-AI-specific) binding law applied to AI, non-binding
policy/ethics guidance, and — in the UAE's case especially — binding
AI-specific rules that exist only at a sub-national/free-zone level rather
than nationally.

## Things worth flagging for the fact-checker

- **UAE is a genuine federalism/jurisdiction puzzle.** There is no federal
  "UAE AI Act," but Dubai (the emirate) has its own binding AI Act since
  March 2026, and the DIFC (a federal free zone with its own legal system)
  has had binding AI-specific rules since January 2026. I classified the
  UAE as "Sectoral or partial binding law" at the country level to reflect
  that real binding AI-specific rules exist somewhere in the UAE, even
  though they don't apply uniformly nationwide. This is a judgment call —
  the fact-checker/design-prompt writer should decide whether the map needs
  a caveat or footnote explaining that "UAE" coverage is uneven by emirate/
  zone, since a CFO reading a single color on a map might assume uniform
  national coverage.

- **Saudi Arabia's most AI-industry-relevant recent move might not be what
  people expect**: a new copyright/IP law (in force Aug 1, 2026) explicitly
  permits reproducing copyrighted works without the author's permission or
  compensation for the purpose of developing AI products — this is a
  notably permissive stance worth flagging as potentially surprising/
  newsworthy for the fact-checker and later design-prompt writer, since it
  cuts against the "governance = restriction" framing.

- **Israel's Ministry of Justice 'Framework Law' discussion** is real but
  very early-stage (not yet a drafted bill) — I described it as
  "under discussion" rather than "pending legislation" to avoid overstating
  its concreteness; this is a good candidate for the fact-checker to
  re-check closer to any actual publication, since it could change Israel's
  category if/when a bill appears.

- **No paywalled or inaccessible sources in this batch.**

## Confidence

All four rows: medium.
